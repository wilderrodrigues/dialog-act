Restaurant recommender study

Thank you for taking part. In this study you will chat with two dialog systems that recommend a restaurant in Cambridge, and tell us what you thought of them. This takes about 20 minutes.

We record your age, gender, how often you use AI and your answers to the questionnaires. Your name is not recorded, the data is stored anonymously and is only used for this research project of Utrecht University. You can stop at any moment without giving a reason.

Instructions

You will talk to two restaurant recommender systems: System A and System B.

  1. You will have three short interactions with each of the recommender systems.
  2. Before every interaction, you will be given a goal. For example: “try to find a restaurant in the north part of town that you like”. The goal will be your starting point.
  3. The recommender system's abilities are tied to its AI model, which has been trained on very limited data. It means that you cannot have a free and fluid conversation with it. The options are limited to the following:
      Food type: Chinese; French; Thai; British; European; and International.
      Area: North; East; South; and West.
      Price range: Cheap; Moderate; and Expensive. If you say something like "a fancy restaurant", the system will try to match you with an expensive one.
  4. Please, limit the conversations to English only. You can end the conversation either after the recommender system have found a matching restaurant or not. For example, it might reply that no matchings were found.
  5. In the case the recommender system finds a restaurant, you may ask further questions about the phone number or address - if those have not been mentioned.
  6. To end the interaction type either "Goodbye" or "Thank you".
  7. After three interactions, you will be asked to answer to ten short questions about the recommender system.
  8. Example of an interaction:
      S: Hello, welcome to the Cambridge restaurant recommendation system. How may I help you?
      U: I am looking for expensive british food in the centre
      S: I found some restaurants matching your preferences. Do you have any additional requirements?
      U: none
      S: Fitzbillies restaurant is a nice expensive british restaurant in the centre.
      U: what is the phone number
      S: The phone number of fitzbillies restaurant is 01223 352500.
      U: address please
      S: Fitzbillies restaurant is located at 51 trumpington street city centre.
      U: post code
      S: The postcode of fitzbillies restaurant is c.b 1.
      U: thank you goodbye
      S: Good bye!

    It keeps saying it does not understand you
    Ask What don't you understand? It then asks its question again. Answer with one or two words, for example north, cheap or Thai.

    It asks about something your goal does not mention
    I don't care, It doesn't matter or any. Or simply pick something you like.

    Is there anything else? or Something else
    It says no restaurant matches your preferences
