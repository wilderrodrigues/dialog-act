Restaurant recommender study

Thank you for taking part. In this study you will chat with two dialog systems that recommend a restaurant in Cambridge, and tell us what you thought of them. This takes about 20 minutes.

We record your age, gender, how often you use AI and your answers to the questionnaires. Your name is not recorded, the data is stored anonymously and is only used for this research project of Utrecht University. You can stop at any moment without giving a reason.

Instructions

You will talk to two restaurant recommender systems: System A and System B.

  1. With each system you have three short conversations.
  2. Before every conversation you get a goal, for example “try to find a restaurant in the north part of town that you like”. The goal is your starting point. Chat with the system in English until you are satisfied and have found a restaurant that suits you. Reaching the goal does not mean the conversation is over: we want to see how you talk with the system.
  3. You may ask about the recommended restaurant, such as its phone number or address.
  4. When you are done, say goodbye (or thank the system) to end the conversation.
  5. After three conversations you answer ten short questions about that system. Then you do the same with the other system.

Goal:

    The conversation has ended.

    Instructions

    What to do when you get stuck

    The system understands short, simple sentences best. Try one of these:

    It keeps saying it does not understand you
    Ask What don't you understand? It then asks its question again. Answer with one or two words, for example north, cheap or Thai.
    You missed what it said
    Can you repeat that?
    It asks about something your goal does not mention
    I don't care, It doesn't matter or any. Or simply pick something you like.
    It asks about additional requirements and you have none
    No
    You do not like the restaurant it suggests
    Is there anything else? or Something else
    It says no restaurant matches your preferences
    Drop a wish your goal does not need, for example any food or I don't care about the food.
    You want to know more about the restaurant
    What is the phone number? or What is the address?
    You are done
    Thank you or Goodbye ends the conversation.
